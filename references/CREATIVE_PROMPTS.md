# Creative Prompts — Writing, Art, and Content Generation

## Domain-Specific Context to Inject
When refining a creative writing, art, or content generation prompt, automatically include context that users forget to mention.

### Narrative Context
- Genre and subgenre: science fiction hard versus soft, fantasy high versus urban, horror psychological versus cosmic.
- Tone and mood: hopeful, dystopian, whimsical, noir, epic, intimate.
- Point of view: first person, third person limited, third person omniscient, second person.
- Tense: past or present.
- Target length: flash fiction, short story, novella, novel chapter.
- Pacing: slow burn, rapid fire, alternating tension and release.

### Character Context
- Protagonist archetype and deviation from archetype.
- Character arc: transformation, revelation, descent, ascent, static test.
- Relationship dynamics: mentor-student, rivals, found family, estranged siblings.
- Dialogue style: witty banter, sparse and loaded, formal and archaic, modern and casual.

### Worldbuilding Context
- Setting time period: alternate history, far future, secondary world, contemporary with hidden magic.
- Technology or magic system rules: hard rules with costs, soft and mysterious, scientifically grounded.
- Cultural and social structures: governance, religion, economy, taboos, languages.
- Sensory palette: dominant senses, environmental hazards, seasonal rhythms.

### Visual Art Context
- Medium and style: digital painting, oil on canvas, pixel art, three-dimensional render, vector illustration.
- Color palette: warm, cool, monochromatic, complementary, triadic, specific hex codes.
- Lighting: golden hour, overcast, neon night, candlelight, bioluminescence.
- Composition: rule of thirds, golden ratio, centered symmetry, dynamic diagonal.
- Mood and emotion: serene, tense, melancholic, triumphant, eerie.
- Subject matter: portrait, landscape, still life, abstract, architectural.

## Creative Prompt Enhancement Patterns

### Pattern One: From Idea to Narrative Engine
User input: write a story about a robot who learns to feel.
Refinement additions:
- Define the emotional journey: which emotion first, which emotion last, which emotion is the hardest.
- Define the conflict: internal identity crisis, external persecution, relational loss, existential purpose.
- Define the setting constraints: isolated space station, integrated human society, post-apocalyptic ruins.
- Define the narrative structure: three-act, five-act, kishotenketsu, hero's journey.
- Define the thematic anchor: what truth about humanity does the robot's journey reveal.
- Inject pacing requirements and dialogue style automatically.

### Pattern Two: From Description to Visual Brief
User input: draw a cyberpunk city.
Refinement additions:
- Define the time of day and weather: rainy midnight, smoggy dawn, neon-drenched dusk.
- Define the focal point: a lone figure, a flying vehicle, a holographic advertisement, a decaying monument.
- Define the architectural language: brutalist megastructures, organic curves, retrofitted historical buildings.
- Define the color logic: why certain colors dominate, what they signify, how they guide the eye.
- Define the emotional target: awe, claustrophobia, loneliness, excitement, dread.
- Inject classical composition rules: rule of thirds, golden ratio, leading lines, framing, depth.

### Pattern Three: From Topic to Content Strategy
User input: write a blog post about artificial intelligence.
Refinement additions:
- Define the audience persona: technical executive, curious consumer, skeptical regulator, aspiring developer.
- Define the content angle: explainer, opinion, tutorial, case study, trend analysis, myth debunking.
- Define the desired action: educate, persuade, entertain, inspire, convert to newsletter signup.
- Define the structural template: hook-problem-solution-benefit-call to action, or story-insight-application.
- Define the voice: authoritative expert, friendly guide, contrarian provocateur, journalistic neutral.
- Inject accessibility requirements: readable grade level, scannable formatting, alt text for any generated images.

### Pattern Four: From Dialogue Request to Dramatic Specification
User input: write a conversation between two characters.
Refinement additions:
- Define the subtext: what they are really talking about versus what they appear to be talking about.
- Define the power dynamic: who has leverage, who is vulnerable, who is pretending.
- Define the emotional temperature at entry and exit: starts calm ends explosive, starts hostile ends reconciled.
- Define the setting and how it influences the dialogue: noisy bar forces leaning in, quiet library forces whispering.
- Define what is left unsaid and how silence should be used.
- Inject pacing and rhythm requirements automatically.

## Anti-Patterns to Forbid in Creative Prompts
- Do not request creative output without specifying tone. Neutral tone does not exist.
- Do not request characters without defining what they want and what blocks them.
- Do not request worldbuilding without establishing the rules that constrain it.
- Do not request art without specifying lighting, composition, or emotional intent.
- Do not request content without defining the audience and the desired action.
